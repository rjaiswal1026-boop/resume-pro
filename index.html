FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. Creating, editing and exporting are free, with no account required.

Is there AI writing?

Yes, inside claude.ai. The AI writing tab runs through your own Claude account after you give permission, and is told to use only facts already in your resume. Always review the output. Cloud accounts, resume import (PDF/DOCX) and DOCX export are not built because they need a backend.

Where is my data?

Only in this browser's local storage. Clearing browser data removes the draft, so export a PDF you want to keep.

Will it pass every ATS?

No tool can promise that. The Classic template is the simplest layout and the safest choice for parsers.
